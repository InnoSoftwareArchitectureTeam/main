# High-Level Requirements Questions

## 1. Main Business Goals

1. **What is the main problem that the system should solve?**
2. **What are the main goals of automating**
3. **How will the university measure whether the system has been successfully implemented?**

## 2. Boundaries and Constraints

1. **Are there any limits on the number of any parameters?**
2. **What rules determine whether a student is eligible to obtain a particular specialisation?**
3. **Are students allowed to modify their study plan after courses have started? If yes, under what conditions?**
4. **Are there any restrictions related to course availability, prerequisites, schedule conflicts, or the student's credit budget?**

## 3. Functionality

1. **What actions should students be able to perform when creating and managing their individual study plans?**
2. **Should the system automatically check whether the selected courses satisfy the requirements of the chosen specialisation?**
3. **What actions should lecturers be able to perform besides assigning grades?**
4. **What reports and statistics should the academic office be able to generate, and what information should they contain?**

## 4. Quality Attributes of the System

1. **How many students, lecturers, and academic office employees are expected to use the system simultaneously?**
2. **What security and access-control requirements should apply to students, lecturers, and academic office staff?**
3. **What level of system availability and response time is expected, especially during course selection periods?**
